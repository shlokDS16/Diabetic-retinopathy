- **F1 figF01_architecture** — NRDI system: the periorbital wearable (thermopile, camera + 940-nm LED, four dry electrodes per eye, temple PPG), the physics digital twin that returns eight design verdicts to the hardware, the feature layer, and the multimodal AI (fundus DR grader, DME head, systemic EHR model, physiological branch) fused ordinally into DR grade, referable DR, DME and DN risk. Bottom: the open datasets behind every result, with mBRSET/BRSET marked pending.
- **F2 figF02_anatomy_sensors** — Frame v3 (path-traced CAD), exploded assembly with part callouts, sagittal section of the 14-tissue finite-element anatomy (122,279 nodes, 705,228 tetrahedra), and the frame on the head with the simulated skin-temperature field.
- **F3 figF03_thermal** — Pennes bioheat solution: sagittal and axial temperature slices with tissue boundaries; change in each readout as choroidal perfusion falls (the 90° thermopile field of view reads a 28 mm spot and captures 28 % of the true corneal deficit; the 5° field of view 14 %); simulated corneal-apex and canthus temperatures against published ranges (both simulated values sit about 1 °C below the published bands at 25 °C ambient).
- **F4 figF04_verification** — Method of manufactured solutions (observed order 1.79, P1 tetrahedra), grid-convergence index per quantity of interest (all below 2 %), and steady-state corneal, lid and canthus heating vs 940-nm LED power up to the IEC 62471 limit of 42.3 mW (0.21 °C at the apex).
- **F5 figF05_uq** — Sobol total-order indices for eight parameters and four readouts: ambient temperature dominates every readout (S_T 0.74–1.01), choroidal perfusion contributes 8–11 % to the ocular readouts; distributions over 640 Saltelli solves; ambient-vs-apex scatter coloured by choroidal fraction (slope 0.32 °C per °C ambient; corrected 2026-09-08).
- **F6 figF06_optical** — Monte Carlo photon-measurement density of the 3 mm temple PPG at 660 and 880 nm (sampled depth 1.1–1.2 mm, arterial share 0.7–2.1 %); arterial share rises to 35 % at 12 mm spacing while detected photons fall 40×; absorbed fraction of 940-nm LED power per tissue (eyelid 14 %, vitreous 13.5 %) and the absorbed field through the globe.
- **F7 figF07_bioimpedance** — Tissue contributions to the tetrapolar transfer impedance at 50 kHz (orbit, eye and lid 15.6 %), |Z| and phase spectra for tetrapolar vs bipolar configurations with reciprocity and Geselowitz residuals below 1e-14, and edema sensitivity (−1.4 % |Z| per +10 % orbital water tetrapolar vs −0.13 % bipolar with contact impedance).
- **F8 figF08_pupil** — Longtin–Milton pupil model: healthy vs reduced autonomic gain traces; amplitude, velocities and latency vs gain; constriction amplitude vs stimulus illuminance showing that a 940-nm illuminator (≈0 photopic lux) evokes no reflex; camera vergence foreshortening (minor axis 0.63 D, naive under-read 37 %).
- **F9 figF09_fundus_dr** — DeepDRiD patient-grouped 5-fold OOF confusion (QWK 0.857 [0.835, 0.878]), referable-DR ROC with bootstrap bands for DeepDRiD OOF (0.97), IDRiD (0.95) and APTOS 2019 (0.98, n = 3,662), ordinal-score distribution per true grade with fitted thresholds, and per-fold validation curves.
- **F10 figF10_generalisation** — QWK, referable AUC and accuracy per dataset with 95 % CIs (DeepDRiD thresholds applied unchanged), sensitivity/specificity at the OOF operating point, fitted vs re-fitted thresholds (APTOS shows a systematic +1-grade shift: QWK 0.845 unchanged thresholds vs 0.878 re-fitted, accuracy 55 %), and grade prevalence per dataset.
- **F11 figF11_dme** — DME head on IDRiD: fixed-test confusion (QWK 0.84 [0.72, 0.92]), any-DME ROC (OOF 0.98, test 0.95), DR × DME co-occurrence on all 516 images (any DME in 91 % of referable vs 0 % of non-referable eyes), and DME vs DR head scores on the test split (r = 0.77).
- **F12 figF12_ehr** — Istanbul EHR (n = 77,724): ROC for retinopathy (boosted AUC 0.720) and diabetic neuropathy (0.710, treatment proxies excluded), calibration, odds ratios per SD, permutation importance, and the joint four-class outcome with the DR–DN odds ratio 1.91. Sensitivity analyses in `tabular_ehr.json["dn_sensitivity"]`: with proxies 0.838; also removing analgesics and psychoanaleptics 0.708.
- **F13 figF13_physio** — HRV separates diabetics from controls (LOO AUC 0.78 [0.67, 0.87], n = 94) but not retinopathy within diabetics (n = 58, all BH-corrected p ≥ 0.84); mean HR correlates with HbA1c (ρ = 0.30, p = 0.03); PPG-BP fingertip PPG carries no diabetes signal beyond demographics (all routes ≤ 0.49) while the same features detect hypertension (0.73); PaPaGei age control r = 0.38 on tiled 2.1-s segments.
